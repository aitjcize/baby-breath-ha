A calm extra pair of eyes

This add-on watches your camera for the gentle rise and fall of breathing motion and shares what it sees with Home Assistant.

This is an experiment, not a guardian.
  • It is not a medical device and not a life-safety system.
  • Never use it as the primary or only way to monitor your baby.
  • Video motion can vanish for many harmless reasons — and a visible signal does not prove your baby is safe.

Connect your camera

Paste the RTSP stream URL of the camera that watches the crib.

Usually found in your camera's app under RTSP, ONVIF, or "local streaming". A wired or strong-signal camera on a fixed mount works best. No camera handy? Enter demo://breathing to explore with a synthetic scene. A camera is already configured — testing a new URL only replaces it when you continue.

Draw the breathing region

Drag a box over where your baby sleeps. Draw it generously — the monitor finds and follows the breathing rhythm within the box, so it keeps working when your baby moves during the night.

The box must contain your baby only. If it can include a co-sleeping adult, the monitor may measure their breathing instead of your baby's.

Connect to Home Assistant

Readings become Home Assistant entities through MQTT. The broker bundled with Home Assistant (the Mosquitto add-on) needs zero setup — or point at a broker running anywhere else on your network.
